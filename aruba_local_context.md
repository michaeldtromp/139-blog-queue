# Aruba local context — get this right

**Aruba sits below the hurricane belt. Hurricanes essentially never hit the island.**
Why: Michael flagged this on 2026-09-03 after a proposed post titled "Hurricane season: the website checklist every Aruba business needs" — his words: "The post are for Aruba, and never happens here.. what hurricane season are you talking about???"
How to apply: never write hurricane-season, storm-prep or evacuation-angle content for the Aruba audience. It reads as ignorant of the island to every local reader and damages the studio's credibility. The same caution applies to any "disaster season" framing borrowed from US/Caribbean-mainland marketing calendars.

**Check every local claim against Aruba specifically, not "the Caribbean" generically.**
Why: Aruba differs sharply from the wider Caribbean on climate, currency (Aruban florin, USD widely accepted), language mix, and tourism patterns.
How to apply: before publishing a local angle — weather, seasonality, regulation, holidays, payment habits — verify it is true for Aruba. When unsure, cut the local claim rather than guess. Michael is Aruban and reads every post; a wrong local detail is worse than no local detail.

**Language mix:** English, Spanish, Dutch and Papiamento are all in daily use. Papiamento is the local language — spell it correctly and never treat it as a dialect of Spanish.
